what I do

I am in my second year in the graphic design Bachelor of Fine Arts Program at the University of Arkansas where I am studying branding, identity systems, typography, user interface/ experiences, interactive language, and design tools and concepts. I also have strong skills in hand lettering, photography, and illustration. I am passionate about creating meaningful design that has intention behind it and expands on itself by creating feeling in my audience. In my free time I love to learn, create, work with people and children, and spend time with my friends and family. Thank you for spending some time looking at my work and getting to know me!
